# Log-Normal Distribution

The **Log-Normal Distribution** is a continuous probability distribution of a random variable whose logarithm is normally distributed. It is frequently used to model natural phenomena and economic data that cannot be negative and are "skewed" toward higher values.

---

## Defining the Relationship

The core identity of a log-normal distribution is defined by its relationship with the **Normal (Gaussian) Distribution**.

### 1. The Logarithmic Transformation

If a random variable **x** is **log-normally distributed**, then applying a natural logarithm to it creates a new variable **y** that follows a **Normal Distribution**.

* **Formula:** y = ln(x)
* **Meaning:** By "logging" the skewed data, you "compress" the long tail, resulting in a symmetric bell curve.

### 2. The Exponential Transformation (Inverse)

Conversely, if a random variable **y** is **normally distributed**, then applying an exponential function to it results in a variable **x** that follows a **Log-Normal Distribution**.

* **Formula:** x = exp(y)

---

## Visual Characteristics

While a Normal Distribution is a symmetric bell curve, the Log-Normal Distribution is a **Right-Skewed Distribution**.

* **Right-Skewed Shape:** The "tail" of the graph is pulled or elongated toward the right side.
* **Impact of Standard Deviation:** While the mean might stay constant, as the standard deviation changes, the right side of the curve becomes more elongated and skewed.
* **Zero Bound:** Unlike the Normal distribution, which can go to negative infinity, a log-normal variable is always positive because the exponential of any number is positive.

---

## Real-World Examples

Data that follows a log-normal distribution is typically characterized by many small values and a few very large "outliers".

| Example | Log-Normal Intuition |
| --- | --- |
| **Wealth Distribution** | Most people belong to the "salaried class" with modest wealth, while a tiny percentage (the "1%") like Elon Musk or Bill Gates hold massive wealth in the long tail. |
| **Comment Lengths** | In discussion forums, most people write short, quick comments. Only a very few people write extremely long, detailed explanations. |
| **Dwell Time** | When reading online articles or news, many users leave quickly (short dwell time), while only a few stay for a very long duration. |
| **Chess Games** | The length of chess games (number of moves) often follows this pattern, with many games ending in a standard range and a few lasting exceptionally long. |

---

## Technical Comparison

| Feature | Normal Distribution | Log-Normal Distribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Shape** | Symmetric Bell Curve | Right-Skewed |
| **Symmetry** | Mean = Median = Mode | Mean > Median > Mode |
| **Values** | Can be negative, zero, or positive | Always positive (> 0) |
| **Verification** | Verified using a **Q-Q Plot** | Verified by taking the log of data, then using a **Q-Q Plot** |
