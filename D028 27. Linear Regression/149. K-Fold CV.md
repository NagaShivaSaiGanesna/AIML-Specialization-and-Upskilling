

### The Complete ML Model Processing Workflow (1,000 Data Points)

**Step 1: The Initial Split (The "Vault")**
Before you even think about K-Fold, you must set aside your **Test Set**.

* **Action:** Take 200 points (20%) and put them in a "vault."
* **Status:** These 200 points are never seen by the model during training or tuning. They are for the final evaluation only.
* **Remaining:** 800 points for the K-Fold process.

**Step 2: Hyperparameter Search & K-Fold (The "Tournament")**
Now, you use those 800 points to find your best "recipe" (parameters). You might test multiple "candidates" (e.g., Tree Depth 5 vs. Tree Depth 10).

* **The Mechanism:** For **each** candidate, you split the 800 points into $K=5$ folds (160 points each).
* **The Rounds:**
* **Round 1:** Train on Folds 2-5, Validate on Fold 1. Record Error $E_1$.
* **Round 2:** Train on Folds 1, 3-5, Validate on Fold 2. Record Error $E_2$.
* *...Repeat for all 5 folds...*


* **The Score:** Calculate the **Average Error** for that candidate: $CV_{(5)} = \frac{1}{5} \sum E_i$.

**Step 3: The Selection (Choosing the Winner)**
You compare the **Average Errors** of all candidates.

* **The Rule:** You don't pick the model from a specific fold. You pick the **Parameters** that produced the lowest/most stable average error across all folds. This proves the settings are robust and not just "lucky" on one slice of data.

**Step 4: Dealing with the "One Category" Risk**

* **The Risk:** Sampling Bias. If a fold accidentally contains only one class, the validation error becomes meaningless.
* **The Solution: Stratified K-Fold.** This forces every fold to mirror the class distribution of the original 800 points (e.g., if the data is 70% "Spam," every fold is 70% "Spam").

**Step 5: The Final Model (The "Grand Reunion")**
Once you have your winning parameters:

* **Action:** Throw away the "mini-models" from the K-Fold rounds.
* **Retrain:** Take your **entire 800-point training set** and train one brand-new model using the winning parameters.
* **Logic:** More data = a stronger model. You've used K-Fold to find the *settings*, now you use the full data to build the *final product*.

**Step 6: The Final Exam**

* **Action:** Bring out the 200 points from the "Vault" (Step 1).
* **Evaluation:** Run them through your Final Model. This is your "unbiased" performance report.
