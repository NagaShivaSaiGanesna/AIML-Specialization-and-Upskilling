# ANOVA Partitioning of Variance (Unicode Version)

The core of ANOVA is dividing the total variation into two components: variance **between** groups and variance **within** groups.

---

## 1. The Statistical Hypotheses

Using Unicode notations, we define our hypotheses as:

* **Null Hypothesis (H₀):** μ₁ = μ₂ = μ₃ = ... = μₖ
* **Alternate Hypothesis (H₁):** At least one sample mean (x̄) is not equal.

---

## 2. Partitioning the Sum of Squares (SS)

To calculate variance, we first find the **Sum of Squares (SS)**. This represents the total "error" or deviation in the data.

**SS_total = SS_between + SS_within**

### Sum of Squares Between (SS_between)

This measures the spread between the group means.

> **Formula:** Σ nᵢ * (x̄ᵢ - x̄_G)²

* **nᵢ** = Sample size of a specific group.
* **x̄ᵢ** = Mean of that specific group.
* **x̄_G** = The Grand Mean (average of all data points).

### Sum of Squares Within (SS_within)

This measures the spread of data points inside each individual group.

> **Formula:** Σ (x - x̄ᵢ)²

---

## 3. Calculating Mean Squares (MS)

To turn these "Sum of Squares" into usable variance values, we divide them by their **Degrees of Freedom (df)**.

1. **Mean Square Between (MS_between):** SS_between / (k - 1)
2. **Mean Square Within (MS_within):** SS_within / (N - k)

* **k** = Number of groups.
* **N** = Total number of observations.

---

## 4. The F-Statistic Calculation

The final **F-value** is the ratio of these two Mean Squares.

**F = MS_between / MS_within**
