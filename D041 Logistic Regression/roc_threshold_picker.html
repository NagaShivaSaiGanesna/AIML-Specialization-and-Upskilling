Step 3 — what you actually do with the threshold

Once picked, apply it to override the default 0.5:
y_pred = (model.predict_proba(X_test)[:,1] >= threshold).astype(int)

Then re-evaluate with a confusion matrix at this threshold — not AUC.
AUC was for comparing models. The threshold is for making decisions.
