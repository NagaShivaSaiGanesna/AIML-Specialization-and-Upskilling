
## AI — Artificial Intelligence
**One liner:** Machines that learn, adapt, and make decisions beyond fixed rules.
**Interview:** AI is the field of building systems that can learn from data, adapt to new situations, and make intelligent decisions — going beyond hardcoded rules or simple automation. It involves reasoning, perception, and problem-solving in ways that improve over time.

---

## ML — Machine Learning
**One liner:** Machines that automatically learn patterns from data without being explicitly programmed.
**Interview:** ML is a subset of AI where algorithms learn patterns from data without being explicitly programmed. Unlike automation, the system improves its own logic as it sees more data — using supervised, unsupervised, or reinforcement learning.

---

## DL — Deep Learning
**One liner:** ML using layered neural networks that automatically learn complex features from raw data.
**Interview:** Deep Learning is a subset of ML that uses multi-layered neural networks to automatically extract increasingly complex features from raw data. Unlike traditional ML, no manual feature engineering is needed — the network learns what to look for on its own.

---

## GenAI — Generative AI
**One liner:** AI that learns patterns from existing data and creates entirely new content.
**Interview:** Generative AI is a class of Deep Learning models that learn the underlying patterns and structure of training data well enough to generate new, realistic content — text, images, audio, or video. It goes beyond classification or prediction — it creates. Built primarily on transformers and diffusion models.

---

## Agentic AI — AI Agents
**One liner:** AI that autonomously plans, makes decisions, and executes multi-step tasks to achieve a goal.
**Interview:** Agentic AI combines LLMs with memory, external tools, and feedback loops to autonomously break down a goal, plan steps, act on them, observe results, and self-correct — with minimal human intervention. It goes beyond responding to a prompt — it completes an entire workflow on its own.

---

**Memory trick:**
> AI *learns* → ML *learns from data* → DL *learns features automatically* → GenAI *learns to create* → Agentic AI *learns to act*
---

## AI but NOT ML
Rule-based systems that are intelligent but don't learn from data.

**Examples:**
- **Chess engine (old Stockfish)** — follows handcrafted rules and search algorithms, no learning
- **Google Maps routing** — uses algorithms and logic, not learned patterns
- **Spam filter with manual rules** — "if email contains 'lottery' → spam"
- **Expert systems in hospitals** — doctors manually coded "if fever + cough → check for flu"

> Intelligent? ✅ Learns from data? ❌

---

## ML but NOT DL
Traditional ML algorithms that learn from data but don't use neural networks.

**Examples:**
- **Linear Regression** predicting house prices
- **Decision Trees** for loan approval
- **Random Forest** for fraud detection
- **SVM** for email classification
- **K-Means clustering** for customer segmentation

> Learns from data? ✅ Uses neural networks? ❌

---

## DL but NOT GenAI
Neural networks that recognize or classify — but don't generate anything new.

**Examples:**
- **Google Photos** recognizing faces — classifies, doesn't create
- **Tesla Autopilot** detecting objects on road — perceives, doesn't generate
- **Cancer detection** from X-rays — diagnoses, doesn't create images
- **Shazam** identifying a song — recognizes, doesn't compose

> Uses deep neural networks? ✅ Generates new content? ❌

---

## GenAI but NOT Agentic
Models that generate content but can't act, plan, or use tools autonomously.

**Examples:**
- **Basic ChatGPT** (no plugins) — answers your question, stops there
- **DALL-E** generating an image — creates on demand, takes no further action
- **GitHub Copilot** suggesting code — generates, but you still decide and act
- **Suno** generating a song — creates audio, does nothing beyond that

> Generates content? ✅ Acts autonomously? ❌

---

---

**Key insight:**
> Every DL is ML. Every ML is AI. But not every AI is ML — and not every ML is DL.
