Why rice is fine — what to actually watch

Rice is not your enemy
1 cup cooked rice = ~200 kcal. Totally fits your 1,900 kcal target. Telugu people have eaten rice forever — it's the portion and the side dishes that matter.
What actually causes fat gain
Excess oil in curries, canteen fried snacks, too many rotis, sugary drinks, eating 3 cups rice instead of 1.
The real rule: protein at every meal
Dal, chicken, eggs, curd, fish, chana — one of these must appear at every meal. This is the only non-negotiable.
Rotate proteins across the week: Mon chicken, Tue dal, Wed egg curry, Thu rajma, Fri fish, Sat paneer — you'll never feel like you're eating the same thing.

Non-negotiables

Protein at every meal, no exceptions
Rotate: chicken, dal, eggs, fish, curd, chana, paneer. One must be on every plate.
Rice stays — but 1 cup per meal max
Don't cut rice. Eat less rice and more protein + sabzi. That's the switch.
3.5+ litres of water daily
Hyderabad heat is real. Carry a bottle always. Dehydration fakes hunger.
Weekend sleep is sacred — 7.5 hrs minimum
You're running on 5.5 hrs weekdays. Weekends must compensate or you'll burn out in week 3.
Pack lunch every single day
Canteen food kills your diet. One dabba = diet on autopilot. No canteen = no guessing.
